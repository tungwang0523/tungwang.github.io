  Today—though at this hour I suppose it was yesterday—the whole family gathered for a New Year dinner at a restaurant, and I drank about 150 millilitres of baijiu.

  Then they coaxed me onto the stage to sing Masao Sen’s “Kitaguni no Haru.” I was, in every sense, barely alive.
  <br/>
  Ah—

  never mind how peculiar it felt to perform before a roomful of relatives I scarcely knew. The moment I picked up the microphone, I pictured Masao Sen singing the song in a rumpled trench coat, a furoshiki bundle over his shoulder: a man from the northern country who had gone to Tokyo to make his way.
  <br/>
  And I could not help thinking of myself out in the wider world. My clothes may be a little smarter, but am I not just such a person too?

今天（这个点来说应该算是昨天了罢）

全家在饭店团拜 喝了三两白酒。

还被撺掇着上台 唱了一首千昌夫的《北国の春》

整个一个活人微死的状态

<br/>

あゝ

先不说在这个全是不怎么熟悉的亲戚的场合当中我的感觉多么微妙

拿起话筒的时候，想起千昌夫唱这首歌的时候，穿着皱皱巴巴的风衣，背着一袋風呂敷，一个去东京打拼的北国人的形象

<br/>

不禁想起自己在外面，虽然可能穿得体面些，不也是这样一个人吗。
