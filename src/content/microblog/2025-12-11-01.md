  I have felt an unusually strong urge to express myself lately.

  Language, after all, marks the limits of thought.

  It has taken me an entire year to understand one thing.
  <br/>
  I was always afraid that I could not hold on to beautiful things, always afraid they would leave me. I obsessed over what changes and what remains: the world around me that I could never quite grasp, a life that refused to settle, the loneliness that never changed. I feared the mutability of things, and the absence of anything dependable.
  <br/>
  A sunset is beautiful. Watching its reflection beside the lake, one wonders what in the world could be lovelier. The sun will set—not because it has abandoned me, but because the Earth is turning. I cannot seize the beauty of a sunset or carry it away. No words, no camera, however well they capture light and shadow, can preserve the whole of the feeling. There is only the present: to feel beauty with my whole being, and happiness with my whole being.

  I came to understand that beauty and happiness are not things to be taken away or left behind, nor must we insist that they endure. Rejoice when they arrive; do not grieve merely because they pass. As long as I exist and remain capable of feeling, I need not fear the endless changes in the people and things around me. It is like the beauty of the sunset: when it appears, I give myself to it; when the sun has gone down, I know that as long as I can return to this lakeside, such splendour will appear again.
  <br/>
  It took me a year—perhaps all twenty-three years of my life—to realise that fearing change and clinging to permanence are both unnecessary. I had thought 2025 the worst year of my life. I spent it immersed in fear and sorrow over change, yet this understanding became the most precious gift the year gave me.
  <br/>
  “Only the clear breeze over the river and the bright moon among the mountains: heard by the ear, they become sound; met by the eye, they take on form. None can forbid us from taking them, and their enjoyment can never be exhausted. They are the Creator’s boundless treasury.” Like Su Shi and his companions drifting upon the Red Cliff river nine hundred and forty-three years ago, “we lay down together in the boat, unaware that the east was already growing light.”

感觉最近表达欲有些强

因为语言是思想的边界

我用一整年的时间想明白了一件事

<br/>

一直害怕自己留不住美好的事物，一直害怕美好的事物离自己而去，我一直在意事物的变与不变——捉摸不透的身边事，无法安定的生活，永远不变的孤独——害怕事物的变化多端，害怕不存在的依靠

<br/>

落日很美，当在湖边欣赏湖边的落日倒影时，会想世间有什么事物比这更美呢？太阳终究会落下，不是因为太阳抛弃了我，而是地球在自转。而落日的美丽我抓不住，带不走，什么文字、什么照相机，即使能捕捉到光影，也留不住这全部的感觉。只有现在，全身心地感受美，全身心地感受幸福。我明白了，美好和幸福是无所谓带走、无所谓留下，无所谓执着它的存在和永恒的——为美丽和幸福的到来而愉悦，而不必为其流走而悲伤，只要我存在着，只要我感受着，对身边人物事物多端变化的害怕是没有必要的，就像落日的美一样——看到日落了，认真感受其美，太阳落下了，我知道只要我还能够走到这湖边，这绝美的景色还会出现。

<br/>

这是我花了一整年，可能是二十三年，想明白的事情——认识到对事物变化的恐惧、对不变的执着是没有必要的。我一直认为2025年是我人生中最糟糕的一年，这一年中我一直沉浸在对变化的恐惧和悲伤当中，但这是2025年送给我的最宝贵的礼物。

<br/>

“惟江上之清风，与山间之明月，耳得之而为声，目遇之而成色，取之无禁，用之不竭，是造物者之无尽藏也。”我和九百四十三年前在赤壁江上泛舟的苏轼和客人一样，“相与枕藉乎舟中，不知东方之既白。”
