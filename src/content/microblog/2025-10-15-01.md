        Good morning, future.

        Morning has come.
        As always, morning has come.

        Expo 2025 closed on October 13, 2025.
        Over its 184 days, more than 25 million people came to visit. Thank you, truly.

        Yes, the Expo has ended.
        But we also believe this: the Expo goes on.
        It goes on within each of us, taking all kinds of new forms.

        Not only as something grand like an idea or an innovation,
        but perhaps as the realization that beyond the sea lie many countries, each with its own culture and values;
        that what we take for granted is not granted at all;
        that while challenges are everywhere, there is just as much hope;
        that humanity, and the world, still have so much room to grow.

        If a feeling that did not exist yesterday has truly been born within you,
        then this morning, and tomorrow morning, can surely lead in an entirely different direction. Surely. Absolutely.

        Morning has come.
        A morning that seems the same as ever, yet is somehow new.
        And so, these are the words we would like to give you:

        Good morning, your future.

        Expo 2025 Osaka, Kansai is now closed.
        Thank you, everyone.
