  The last time I needed a change of mood, I rearranged my desk.
  <br/>
  There is less on it than before—especially the model trains. An entire tabletop once stood crowded with them; now only two remain.
  <br/>
  A CRH5A Hexie EMU: high-speed rail has only just reached my hometown, though somehow it still runs with a model nearly twenty years old.

  An ordinary passenger train hauled by an NY6 diesel locomotive: the NY6 is an obscure model—which is precisely why I bought it; nobody else wanted one, so there was no scramble. Yet through the 1980s and ’90s it was the principal locomotive on the mountain section of the Beijing–Baotou Railway. Perhaps this was the very kind of train my parents’ generation once took when travelling far from home, or returning to it.

  Keeping these two trains may still have something to do with where I come from.

<br/>

尤其是火车模型

以前摆了一桌子

现在只留了两列

<br/>

CRH5A和谐号——老家刚通了高铁，虽然说怎么跑的还是这快20年前的车型；

NY6内燃机车牵引的普速列车——虽然说NY6这个车型很冷门（这也是我为什么买这台机车的原因，没人买，不用抢），但它是八九十年代京包铁路北京山区段的主力牵引机车，说不定父母辈之前坐火车出远门或者回老家的时候坐的就是这一列；

留这两列火车，可能还是和家乡有些关系。
