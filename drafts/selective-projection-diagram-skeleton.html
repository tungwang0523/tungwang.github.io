Design-side expression
Gaze moves toward a person and may remain.
Retain + Relocate
Object shows source, direction, arrival, persistence, withdrawal — stops between the participants, not at the body.
Two gaze paths meet when gaze is returned.
Retain + Reorganize
Two objects merge at the common relation position; split when contact ends.
Prolonged gaze may be construed as pressure or intrusion.
Use as constraint
Objects stay outside both domains; sharpness stays low.
Gaze may be construed as carrying affect, intention, evaluation.
Partly retain
Objects keep an interior; content is never filled, labelled, or classified.
Concentrated attention may make a person feel illuminated or exposed.
Exclude
The person is never spotlighted; changes appear in the relation.
Return gaze may be construed as catching the prior watcher.
Reduce to geometry
Reciprocity visible without accountability, embarrassment, or blame.
